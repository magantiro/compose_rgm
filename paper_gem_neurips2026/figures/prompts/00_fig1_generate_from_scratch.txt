Create a wide landscape scientific figure for a machine-learning paper, in the
visual style of the Chatterjee lab's Schrodinger-bridge figures (BranchedSBM,
EntangledSBM, PepTune). Vector-clean, flat, no photorealism, no 3D, no shadows.

CANVAS AND CONTAINERS
Very pale lavender rounded-rectangle background (#F7F0FB) filling the canvas.
Inside it, three equal-width white rounded-rectangle panel cards with hairline
light-violet borders and generous internal padding, side by side. 16:9.

COLOR: one hue family only, no other accent colors.
  deep navy   #1B1B8F  panel letters, headings, filled equation boxes
  indigo      #6B6BD6  primary molecule nodes, primary arrows
  cyan        #4EC3E0  secondary nodes
  orchid      #C46BD6  highlight / goal
  plus 10-20% tints of these for fills and soft halos

MOLECULES: draw every molecule as a small abstract node-link graph -- filled
circles of slightly varying radius in indigo/cyan/violet, connected by thin
straight bonds. No chemical letters, no atom labels. Each molecule sits in its
own small white rounded tile.

THE CRITICAL CONSTRAINT: the SAME molecule x and the SAME five successor
molecules y1...y5 appear in all three panels, drawn identically and in
IDENTICAL vertical positions. Only the arrows change between panels. This
repetition is the entire point of the figure; do not vary the molecules.

PANEL A -- heading "Executable support" in bold navy, and beneath it the word
"POSSIBLE" large in indigo.
Left: molecule x inside a soft lavender circular halo.
Then TWO distinct layers, left to right:
  layer 1: a vertical column of seven small dots labeled "edit marks"
  layer 2: the five molecule tiles y1...y5 labeled "molecular successors"
Thin dashed arrows fan from x to the seven marks, then from the marks to the
five molecules. Two of the marks must converge onto the SAME single molecule
tile, visibly merging -- this shows that different edits can produce the same
molecule. All arrows equal thickness and equal opacity; no numbers anywhere.
Tag each successor tile with a tiny badge: "+1", "-1", "0", "0", "ring".

PANEL B -- heading "Learned reference process", beneath it "PLAUSIBLE" large in
indigo. Small caption under the heading: "goal-independent".
Same x, same five molecules, same positions. Now single solid curved arrows
from x to each molecule, with arrow WIDTH proportional to these numbers, which
are printed beside each arrow: 0.31, 0.23, 0.16, 0.19, 0.11.
Small horizontal gradient legend bar at the bottom, pale to saturated indigo,
labeled "low" and "high".

PANEL C -- heading "Finite-horizon control", beneath it "PURPOSEFUL" large in
indigo. Small caption: "goal- and budget-conditioned".
At the top of this panel, a FILLED DEEP-NAVY ROUNDED BOX WITH WHITE TEXT
containing the formula. This filled navy equation box is the signature element
of the style; it must be dark navy with white text, not a pale box.
Left side: a small white rounded box outlined in indigo containing a symbol,
with two dashed arrows entering it from above, labeled "goal" and "remaining
budget". These two dashed arrows must point ONLY into that box and must never
touch the arrows coming from x.
Same x, same five molecules, same positions. Curved solid arrows from x to each
molecule, but now with DIFFERENT widths from panel B: the third molecule gets
by far the thickest arrow, the fifth gets the thinnest.
To the right of each molecule, a small faint branching tree of tiny dots
suggesting reachable futures, sized to match, labeled: "weak", "moderate",
"high", "moderate", "dead end". The "high" tree ends in a filled orchid star.
The "dead end" tree ends in a small x mark.
Bottom legend: a pale thin arrow labeled "reference (frozen)" and a thick dark
arrow labeled "control".

TYPOGRAPHY: clean geometric sans, generous whitespace, no gridlines, no drop
shadows, no gradient backgrounds, no 3D, no clip art, no decorative icons.
