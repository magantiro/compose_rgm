# Train-content breadth for scaffold decoration

## Scientific identity and scope

The completed joint-mass decoration development panel emitted 200 valid,
prompt-faithful molecules with 37.0% quality, 97.0% uniqueness and 0.55655
within-prompt diversity. Its frozen 0.56 diversity gate failed. The selected
endpoints were usually unique, and the unique model-supported offer pool had
mean diversity near 0.56. The hypothesis is therefore that broadening
training-supported content proposals within each chosen pendant size/ring cell
increases endpoint diversity without materially lowering quality.

The generated object remains a complete executable COMPOSE program and its
committed scaffold-decoration endpoint. The only proposed intervention is
uniform sampling among the distinct, split-first training pendants in the
already selected attachment-context/size/ring cell. The frozen law uses weights
proportional to the square root of each pendant's source-balanced training
weight. Both laws retain the same joint total-mass distribution, cell
allocation, interfaces, eight-offer panel size, frozen learned-score selector,
executor, checkpoint, evaluator and molecular support. Every previously
supported content entry retains positive mass. No QED, SA, benchmark output,
target drug, comparator or prompt-specific heuristic enters the proposal law.

This revision prepares and tests an optional sampler only. It does not modify
the completed joint-mass result, the running linker/motif evaluations or the
running linker cell-allocation pilot. It makes no oracle or docking call and
does not launch a scored development panel.

The molecular support remains the declared 40-active-atom, 48-slot,
32-primitive, eight-block non-stereochemical graph process. The content catalog
and structural mass prior must retain their sealed split-first training hashes.

## Proposed prospective comparison

A separately authorized, self-hashed matched pilot would compare `frozen` and
`uniform_within_cell` on all ten pinned scaffold-decoration prompts, fresh seed
6, 20 complete attempts per prompt and arm, eight offers per attempt, one CPU
worker and the same frozen model. Lock all 400 selected outputs before applying
the pinned official evaluator. Record every content draw and its probability,
exact execution, learned score, selected endpoint, RNG state, refusal, duration,
chemical validity and prompt fidelity. Missing outputs remain in denominators.

The promotion gate requires at least 190 of 200 outputs and 100% chemical
validity and exact prompt fidelity in each arm. `uniform_within_cell` must
reach mean diversity at least 0.56 and improve the matched frozen arm by at
least 0.01, while losing no more than two quality points and retaining at
least 90% uniqueness. A failed gate is retained as a negative result. Passing
this development gate only qualifies the sampler for fresh independent seeds;
it does not revise the previous failed result or authorize a benchmark claim.

The earlier conditional-content and source-coupled proposals remain separate
negative or unpromoted development observations. Combining their mechanisms
with uniform content sampling would require another prospective contract.
Launching this scored pilot requires separate authorization bound to its exact
contract payload hash.
