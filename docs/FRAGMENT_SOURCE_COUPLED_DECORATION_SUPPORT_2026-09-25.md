# Training-source coupling for scaffold decoration: zero-quality support gate

## Scientific question and scope

The frozen joint-mass decoration sampler plans total added mass across every
declared scaffold interface, then draws pendant content independently within
each site-size/ring category. The completed development result was 37.0% quality,
97.0% uniqueness, 0.55655 diversity and 100% validity. It missed the frozen
0.56 diversity floor. A subsequent train-context-conditioned content pilot made
no quality gain. The remaining hypothesis is that content chosen from cuts of
one training molecule may form more coherent multi-site completions than
independent draws. This is a hypothesis about a content proposal, not a claim
that the cuts form an observed, nonoverlapping decoration bundle.

The generated object remains a complete exact-executor COMPOSE program and its
committed molecular endpoint. The intended comparison is the unchanged
joint-mass proposal against an optional source-coupled content proposal, using
the same frozen reference checkpoint, executor, eight-offer selector, benchmark
prompts and evaluator. The declared support remains the existing 40-active-atom,
48-slot, 32-primitive, eight-block, non-stereochemical graph representation.
No QED, SA, benchmark drug identity or target endpoint is used to fit the
proposed content law.

## Proposed law

The source-coupled sampler first draws total decoration mass and site
size/ring categories from the unchanged train-only joint-mass law. A fixed
half-probability lane then samples a source row uniformly among training
molecules that contain at least one cut matching every sampled category, and
draws one compatible pendant per site from that row. The other half retains
independent content draws. If there is no common source row, the proposal
falls back to the independent draw. The independent lane preserves positive
probability for every previously supported content combination. Every resulting
program still passes the existing exact compiler and executor.

## Pre-scoring structural evidence

The read-only support census consumed the 200 previously sealed joint-mass
development attempts and the same split-first training catalog and mass prior.
Among 1,478 model-supported offers, 1,409 had at least one training source row
with compatible cuts at every required site. In 200 unscored content plans per
prompt, the current independent proposal produced 1,859 distinct content plans
summed over prompts, and the half-coupled proposal produced 1,724. The latter
is a 7.3% drop in this structural diversity diagnostic, not a measurement of
molecular uniqueness or benchmark diversity. Coupling therefore has broad
structural support but a concrete diversity risk, especially given the current
decoration result's narrow diversity miss.

These observations do not authorize a scored pilot or promotion. The next gate
is a bounded, zero-quality, model-scored exact-execution smoke that records
output rate, prompt fidelity, native learned-score eligibility and program
family usage for both arms. A subsequent scored comparison would require a
separate self-hashed, prospectively frozen matched contract and explicit launch
authorization. The ongoing official motif/linker runs and the matched linker
cell-broadening pilot remain immutable.
