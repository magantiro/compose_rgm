# T4 held-target route-distillation quality gate

This zero-oracle gate trains the existing route-policy comparison on four T4
targets and evaluates on the fifth. All three source seeds for the held target
are excluded from fitting. The compiler, candidate generator, and offline
diagnostic separation are unchanged. The three reported arms are the generic
source-balanced marginal, the held-target structural actor, and the
held-target graph-conditioned hybrid ranker.

Teacher-injected panels remain labeled rank diagnostics. They do not establish
autonomous recovery. A scored launch requires a later payload-bound contract
that records the frozen fold models, exact backbone, candidate budgets, and
oracle policy.
