# Attachment-specific complete-program proposal

## Current implementation unit

Problem: useful complete transformations remain improbable when their attachments
and dependent primitive choices are sampled independently. Output: a bounded edit
program with typed input/created-atom references, its legal attachment assignment,
and an exact primitive execution receipt. Hypothesis: context-matched complete
programs improve executable proposal density without changing molecular support.

This is an optional optimization proposal over the existing executor, not a new
reference law or a trained Doob controller. Its first retrieval implementation
uses a finite bank of observed, parameter-bound programs, while the unchanged
broad/generic channel retains chemistry outside that bank. It is not yet an
open-ended learned parameter decoder.
Program extraction learns demonstrated transformations, not synthetic reactions.
All original broad/generic pathways remain unchanged. No scientific claim of
reference absolute continuity is made without actual probability evaluation.

The first local experiment compares attachment-context ranking with unconditioned
binding over the same extracted programs and binding/work caps. Reuse the prior
eleven/four source roles before extraction. Measure complete exact replay,
canonical diversity, transferred reconstruction, unsupported/exhausted counts,
executor applications and wall time. A reconstruction is supporting evidence;
an unscored product is not a docking improvement or a negative docking example.

Caps: at most five declared blocks and 24 primitive edits per complete program;
32 attachment assignments and 4096 binding-search visits per program/source.
The search cap is explicit proposal computation, not a reduction of executor
support. Report truncated binding searches rather than claiming an exhaustive
attachment census. No new oracle call, Modal job, or model fit in this unit.

## Acceptance and decisions

- Extract from existing exact states/actions, validating every stored state.
- Bind later operations to the specific atoms created earlier, including when
  a deleted persistent slot is reused by a later birth.
- Execute only through the production executor and codec. Reject malformed,
  incompatible, over-budget and invalid programs without endpoint substitution.
- Never store a winner SMILES or full desired endpoint in the inference program.
- Preserve complete-option boundaries and exact replay; intermediate endpoint
  infeasibility is not a pruning rule. Protected slots are explicit.
- Test multi-option dependencies, transfer to a different context, slot relabeling,
  stale/deleted references, serialization and non-mutating failure.
- Public scored pools retain source/run/row and oracle-domain provenance. Rows
  are observations, not trajectories or propensity-bearing policy advantages.

Positive local result: prepare the separately authorized, fully scored proposal
versus selector assay. Negative transfer: repair program/context coverage before
fitting a larger actor. Missing task-score contrast: mine genuine public or paid
alternatives, do not label unscored variants. The suggested 66-call experiment is
authorized by the subsequent approval, but has not launched. Do not relabel
undersized pools as that experiment. Looser KL and a neural pointer decoder are
not enabled. The separate nineteen-call refinement remains a different assay.

## Multi-site extension and current evidence

The user subsequently requested coordinated edits across disconnected sites.
`edit_program_graph.py` compiles created-atom dependencies, conservative operand
conflicts and separate serialization edges. It preserves overlapping dependent
edits instead of excluding them. Complete blocks execute serially, with peak
capacity checked against forty active atoms and every primitive revalidated by
the unchanged executor. A conflict-free graph is not a chemical safety certificate.
Failed programs produce receipts, not substitute endpoints.

Programs use slot-independent input/created handles. All intermediate molecules
remain connected and valid; no intermediate docking, QED, SA or similarity cutoff
prunes a program. Actual sites are source-graph connected components of net
changed original-atom environments, not the size of the intended mutable region.

`multi_site_program_policy.py` implements three isolated proposal arms:
uninterrupted serial programs, independently drawn multi-site bundles, and joint
retrieval of complete coordinated programs. These are callable proposal channels,
not yet integrated into the deployed population controller. The joint arm requires
a separately prepared coordinated-program bank. No task-adapted decoder or
future-value model was trained for these probes.

Measured local results, RDKit 2024.03.5, zero new oracle calls:

- Attachment transfer: 38 train-derived programs, 24 previously excluded-source
  diagnostic parents, 32 attempts per parent/arm. Context matching completed
  636/768 versus 392/768 unconditioned; canonical counts summed over parents were
  528 versus 364. Both reconstructed 5/24 targets. Preparation, transfer and the
  public-source census took 34.94 seconds. This is retrospective cross-source
  structural evidence, not task improvement.
- Public evidence: 87,448 rows across thirty released T4 files were inventoried
  against immutable upstream identities. Scores remain external observations,
  not COMPOSE-policy continuation labels.
- Exact route: recorded and core-first schedules execute the same 21 primitives
  and reach the same PARP1 molecule. Dependencies are linker -> pendant -> fusion
  -> peripheral refinement; the core block can precede that chain in this example.
  Both schedules change two disconnected source-induced sites. The assay took
  0.79 seconds, with no claim of equal reference path probabilities.
- Answer-known proposal assay: 32 attempts per arm/context with identical
  24-primitive and 120-second ceilings; five blocks from the seed, four from the
  post-linker state. Actual work differs and is reported. After correcting the
  controls' horizons and providing complete peripheral/core branches, joint
  retrieval proposed the demonstrated endpoint 14/32 times from the seed versus
  4/32 serial and 2/32 independent. Post-linker counts are 7/32 joint, 3/32 serial,
  and 1/32 independent. Joint pools contain three and six unique eligible endpoints, so
  neither fills an eight-candidate pool. This uses the route in preparation and
  is not held-out learning or autonomous discovery.

but training density remains narrow. Expand source-grouped multi-site structural
examples and genuinely scored program contrasts, including linker/core changes,
