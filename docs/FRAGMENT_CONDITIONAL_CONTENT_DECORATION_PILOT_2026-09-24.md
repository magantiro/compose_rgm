# Conditional-content decoration pilot

## Scientific question

The completed joint-mass decoration pilot produced 200/200 valid and faithful
outputs, 37.0% quality, 97.0% uniqueness and 0.556547 diversity. Its quality
reached the published InVirtuoGen value, but diversity fell just below 0.56.
The sampler plans total added mass jointly but chooses the molecular content of
each pendant from a global train-derived catalog. This pilot tests whether
conditioning only that within-size-and-ring-category content choice on observed
training-molecule core size and interface count improves quality without
reducing output, fidelity or diversity.

The generated object is a complete valid molecular graph assembled by exact
primitive COMPOSE programs at the scaffold's legal interfaces. The frozen
RingCore checkpoint, executor, eight-offer model selection, joint size law,
prompt set and official evaluator are shared across arms. The new law uses no
QED or SA guidance and no benchmark prompt or drug identity to fit weights.
The training-derived catalog retains acyclic, branched and ring-containing
pendants within the declared atom/charge/size and executor support.

## Pre-scoring support evidence

The observed whole-Murcko-decoration-bundle idea was rejected without scoring:
the exact train-only bundle support was sparse and included no ring-containing
pendant, because rings were assigned to the Murcko core. The broader bridge-cut
catalog preserved ring-containing support. The conditional-content planner
produced 151-200 distinct site-bound plans per 200 draws across the ten prompts.
Its exact-execution check yielded 26/30 valid faithful outputs, compared with
28/30 for the existing law. In a full eight-offer model panel, it yielded
20/20 valid faithful outputs, with 146/160 model-supported offers. These are
zero-quality support checks, not benchmark results.
