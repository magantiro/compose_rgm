# T4 target-conditioned utility plus Dynamic-v0 qualification

## Decision frozen by this revision

This revision freezes the smallest prospective test of the missing objective
signal. One target-conditioned utility selector chooses a complete macro jump
from the same immutable candidate universe used by the target-blind structural
selector. The structural selector contributes a one-query control. The utility
jump becomes call 1 of one run-local archive, after which the existing
Dynamic-v0 refinement adapter runs unchanged.

This successor contract makes one narrow change to the parent integration
boundary: the utility selector may consume the frozen five-level target
condition. Target identity remains prohibited in macro generation, exact
realization, common eligibility, archive admission and Dynamic-v0 refinement.
All other parent invariants remain binding.

The five development cells are `5ht1b_0`, `braf_1`, `jak2_1`, `parp1_0` and
`fa7_0`. The initial qualification, if separately authorized, ends at 100
charged utility-plus-v0 calls per cell and one structural-control call per cell,
for at most 505 new charged calls. There is no plateau stopping, retry,
confirmation call or replacement candidate. Best-so-far docking utility is
reported at calls 1, 5, 10, 20, 50 and 100. Any later extension must be
separately authorized and must also report its predeclared later checkpoints
and final call.

## Promotion rules

The utility jump first has to beat the target-blind structural jump on the
complete five-cell paired panel. Its cell-balanced mean docking-score difference
must be strictly negative, it must win at least three cells, and all five pairs
must be observed. On the three cells with historical Dynamic-v0 curves, the
utility jump must also meet or beat the bound call-1 value.

The integrated utility-plus-v0 controller then has to meet or beat Dynamic-v0
pointwise at calls 1, 5, 10 and 20 on all three referenced cells. Averaging may
not hide a regression. On the referenced hard case `jak2_1`, its nonnegative
gap to Full-146 must be no larger than Dynamic-v0's gap at calls 50 and 100 and
strictly smaller at one or both calls. FA7 remains an explicit missing-reference
abstention, so even a passing JAK2 result cannot support a general hard-case or
FA7 claim.

A good first molecule is therefore insufficient. Promotion requires the
one-shot, early-trajectory and hard-case gap gates together. The numerical
comparators were measured before this utility model. The remaining rules use
zero-margin non-inferiority or strict improvement, not a threshold fitted after
seeing the new model.

## Scope and next authority

This contract authorizes zero oracle calls, zero docking calls, zero Modal
launches and zero live-run reads. It changes no frozen artifact. Before a launch
can be requested, a supplement must bind the exact trained fold checkpoints,
five-cell candidate and admission lock, independent random states, evaluator
inputs and clean launch revision. The user must then explicitly authorize that
exact hash and a bounded call ceiling.
