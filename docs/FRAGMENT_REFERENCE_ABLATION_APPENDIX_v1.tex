\subsection{Reference-probability ablation in superstructure generation}
\label{app:fragment-reference-ablation}

We tested the learned edit-selection probabilities in the completed
superstructure sampler. The comparison law samples uniformly among available
rewrite families and then uniformly among masked native mark coordinates in
the selected family. Both arms use the same native action masks, structural
controls, learned hazard, stopping rules, executor, and benchmark evaluator.
The comparison is not uniform over distinct canonical successors.

Across the ten prompts, the mean quality difference was 13.63 percentage
points. A percentile bootstrap that resampled prompts after averaging the
three seeds within each prompt gave a 95\% interval of 4.27 to 23.07 points.
The corresponding intervals were 1.07 to 11.70 points for uniqueness and
-0.00052 to 0.03111 for diversity. Generated molecules within a prompt were
not treated as independent structural problems. The learned law improved
quality on six prompts, trailed on two, and tied on two
(\cref{tab:fragment-reference-prompts}). Seed labels match between arms,
but their random draws do not pair molecular trajectories.

The comparison isolates learned edit-selection probabilities in the
superstructure primitive sampler. The learned hazard remains active in both
arms. It does not test the complete reference against a wholly unlearned
generator or establish an effect for the finite-panel program controllers
used in other fragment tasks.

\paragraph{Selection on recorded linker program panels.}
As a separate supporting comparison, we replayed learned and uniform endpoint
selection on the 3,000 saved eight-offer panels from the completed prior
linker method. Proposal construction, native-support admission, structural
restrictions, and the evaluator were unchanged. Learned selection gave
28.6\% quality, 72.4\% uniqueness, and 0.562149 diversity. Uniform selection
gave 23.333\% quality, 73.367\% uniqueness, and 0.573898 diversity. Both
arms retained 100\% chemical validity. The mean learned-minus-uniform
quality difference across ten prompts was 5.267 points, with a 95\%
prompt-bootstrap interval of 1.967 to 9.133 points. This conditional
program-panel comparison does not remove the training-derived linker catalog
or test the entire transition reference. It applies to the recorded linker
configuration and is not transferred to a later linker or morphing method.
