00

The thesis

One sentence

COMPOSE instantiates Rewrite Generator Matching: stochastic chemical graph rewriting defines the legal molecular state graph and executable events, while Generator Matching learns their contextual firing rates—yielding a target-free, flexible-size generator that is sufficiently competitive de novo and designed to excel at valid conditional generation and optimization.

The field has quietly told us where the value is. Morph's flexible-size 3D model is most compelling in steering and out-of-distribution design; GrIDDD leads with property targeting and optimization. We do not turn this paper into an FCD arms race. The unconditional model must be valid, diverse, faithful, and covering enough to support downstream design. The headline is the regime where executable editing matters most: conditional generation and optimization over complete valid molecules.

The added intellectual contribution

This is not merely an operator library. RGM specializes Generator Matching to state-dependent admissible matches of a molecular rewrite system. Certified endpoint-conditioned rewrite programs supply conditional generators; the generic conditional-to-marginal and latent-program projection identities are inherited from Generator Matching and Latent Process Generator Matching. COMPOSE contributes the chemically admissible bridge and support, contextual rates over complete connected molecules, and coordinated executable marks; inference ancestrally samples those learned rates with no target, compiler, beam search, or terminal repair.

02

Four contributions

C1 · Structured specialization

Rewrite Generator Matching

We specialize Generator Matching to admissible matches of a stochastic molecular rewrite system: qualitative rules and application conditions define which jumps exist; a neural marked-rate model learns when and where they fire; executable aliases are summed at the canonical chemical successor.

C2 · Event construction

A validity-closed, coordinated molecular process

The production vocabulary is grow/shrink · atom restate · bond reorder · subtree Graft · whole ring-system grow/delete. Every committed successor passes the same valence, sanitization, and connectivity boundary in training and inference. Graft and whole-ring marks are atomic derived rewrites with verified primitive lowerings—not unstructured multi-edit bundles.

C3 · Bridge design

Transport-aware construction and editing

A directly samplable carbon-tree source, flexible-size coupling, Graft path compression, and whole-ring events are designed to shorten certified programs and reduce delete-to-one-and-regrow supervision. Whole-ring commitment coordinates cyclic topology atomically; whether it should occur early or late is an empirical scheduling ablation, not an assumed defect. A matched macro-versus-micro ablation must establish that this inductive bias improves learnability; the scoped path-ambiguity analysis predicts that correction is easiest in the small-transport conditional regime.

C4 · Result

Sufficient de novo; headline conditional

First establish a faithful unconditional base with pathwise validity. Then test the paper's strongest empirical claim: property targeting and molecular optimization at 100% committed-state validity, with matched-sample efficiency and coverage rather than unsupported promises of SOTA before the runs exist.

03

The companion theory: transport and program ambiguity

Rewrite Generator Matching is the primary intellectual contribution. The transport analysis explains which source–target couplings make its rate-learning problem easy or hard and why conditional editing is the natural high-value regime.

The scoped result to prove and test. Under a single-successor or sparsely sampled program teacher, let Meff be the number of exchangeable legal edits that make comparable progress from the same state. When the compiler selects one of those edits, positive teacher mass per exchangeable successor scales like 1/Meff under the stated symmetry assumptions. Larger transport generally increases path multiplicity and target ambiguity; small-transport correction reduces both. This is a prediction about the coupling and teacher—not an architecture-independent impossibility theorem.

How it changes the design

We do not conclude that large-transport editing is impossible or that per-coordinate models cannot approximate coordinated changes. We hypothesize a learnability advantage from shorter certified programs: source trees are valid and directly samplable; Graft can preserve useful subtrees; whole-ring-system events coordinate ring topology in one executable mark; randomized causal teachers expose more than one valid next correction; and a separate recovery curriculum supplies small-transport editing examples. Ring-event timing is tested separately because late commitment can be sensible once an acyclic scaffold is established. The confirmatory experiment bins states by path length, effective branching, and teacher successor entropy and includes matched macro-versus-micro interventions.

04

The method

ProcessStochastic rewriting supplies the generator support

Let Mc be the complete, connected molecular graphs admitted by chemistry and hard condition c, and let Ac(G) be the legal marked rewrite matches at state G. COMPOSE learns the jump generator

(Lθ,t,cf)(G) = Σa∈Ac(G) qθ,t(a|G,c) [ f(TaG) − f(G) ].

The rewrite layer determines rules, matches, application conditions, executors, exact inverses, and the legal state graph. The network learns only non-negative context- and time-dependent rates. If symmetric marks or different executable aliases reach the same canonical molecule, their rates are summed at that successor.

VocabularyExpressive micro rules + verified derived rules

The semantic micro basis contains atom insertion/deletion, atom restatement, and bond insertion/deletion/reordering. The production marked model promotes two preidentified coordination bottlenecks into atomic derived events:

  • Subtree Graft atomically cuts one bridge and reconnects the detached component across the cut, preserving connectivity and useful source topology.
  • Whole ring-system grow/delete jointly commits or removes a complete single, fused, bridged, spiro, or mixed cyclic component, including its bond orders, aromatic semantics, atom labels, charges, and hydrogens.

The production generator contains no learned ring ear and no independent scalar ring closure. A whole-ring mark is one chemically coordinated rewrite, not an arbitrary bundle of simultaneous edits. The implementation may use a versioned, training-derived support catalog for topology and electronic payloads, but each entry is a parameterized executable rewrite with a validity-checked micro lowering and exact inverse semantics—not a terminal fragment token. Catalog scope, split-wise coverage, and misses are reported explicitly.

TrainingCertified programs become Generator Matching teachers

Training samples a target-independent, degree-bounded carbon tree from a declared source distribution and a molecule from the data distribution. A flexible-size compiler grows, shrinks, Grafts, restates, reorders, and commits complete ring systems until the exact endpoint is reached through legal states. A progress CTMC on each certified program provides conditional firing rates. The neural model receives only the current molecule and time. Marginalizing the privileged endpoint and latent program follows the general identities of Generator Matching and Latent Process Generator Matching; the COMPOSE obligation is to construct a chemically admissible bridge and an exact or unbiased conditional target on that bridge.

The rate model factorizes total hazard, rewrite family, topology or operand, legal match, and payload. Hard masks come from the rewrite executor; learned scores determine probability only within the legal fiber. This is why a corpus-frequency base measure for common ring topologies does not eliminate learning: it initializes support frequencies, while the network learns the contextual residual.

InferenceTarget-free ancestral CTMC sampling

At inference, sample a fresh source tree from the same source marginal, evaluate the learned hazard and marked rewrite distribution, draw one legal event, commit it, and repeat. There is no target molecule, compiler, beam search, candidate bank, or terminal repair. The visible trajectory always consists of complete connected molecular graphs admitted by the declared molecular state space.

De novoA sufficiency gate, not the paper's final battleground

The random carbon tree is structured noise, not a scaffold that must survive and not fragment assembly. Flexible-size and recovery supervision expose grow and shrink; Graft is intended to reduce delete-to-one compiler paths; atom restatement and bond/ring events make non-monotone revision available. Whether the learned process actually uses these affordances instead of behaving like any-order construction is an empirical operator-use and trajectory question. We advance once the unconditional process is faithful, diverse, non-memorizing, covering, and computationally tractable. An earlier grow-model FCD near 10 is historical motivation, not a result of the final RGM system.

TransferOne generator, plus a principled recovery curriculum

A construction-only teacher does not by itself prove local molecular editing. The final training program therefore combines two explicitly logged trajectory distributions:

  • Base transport — independently sampled valid tree → corpus molecule; trains target-free de-novo generation, variable size, Graft, retyping, bond revision, and whole-ring construction.
  • Recovery / revision — apply k≈1–4 valid perturbations to a data molecule and learn the inverse causal rewrite distribution; supplies direct supervision for shrink, ring-system delete, Graft reversal, and small-transport correction.

The base and recovery mixtures share the same state space, executor, rate parameterization, and ancestral sampler. Their mixture weight is an ablation, and operator-use audits must show that editing capability is actually learned rather than inferred from reachability alone.

ConditionalOne model, three levers on a spectrum

The same valid rewrite generator is steered three ways as the target gets rarer:

  1. Property conditioning (classifier-free guidance). Feed target y as an input and drop it on ~15% of batches so one model supports conditional and unconditional sampling. Benchmark against FreeGress-style targeting under the matched protocol.
  2. Modular inference-time guidance. Tilt only rates of legal successor rewrites using a frozen oracle or learned value function. The first baseline enumerates or samples valid successors locally; a Doob/value approximation is promoted only if it improves compute–quality tradeoffs.
  3. Reward fine-tuning. Fine-tune the marked CTMC toward a frozen reward with a KL or trajectory anchor to the base. Hard chemistry support is unchanged, but oracle exploitation, diversity collapse, and applicability-domain escape remain empirical risks.
05

Experiments & the numbers to beat

Seven result blocks. E3 is the empirical headline; E0 establishes that the structured molecular construction is mathematically and computationally well posed, and E1 establishes a credible generator. E1 remains a sufficiency gate, not an endless battleground.

E0 · Rewrite Generator Matching contract — prove the method is what we claim

  • Closure and well-posedness: prove that every legal rewrite preserves the declared state space; state the finite atom bound, finite-match conditions, and rate assumptions that imply finite total hazard and non-explosion. Empirically audit every committed training and sampling state with the frozen valence grammar, independent RDKit sanitization, and connectivity checks.
  • Bounded reachability: characterize the communicating class induced by the micro rules and show reachability between the declared bounded source and target domains. State explicitly which chemistry falls outside that domain.
  • Bridge correctness: prove that each certified program defines a valid endpoint-conditioned generator and that the implemented teacher is exact or unbiased for its conditional Generator Matching target. Report source–target coupling coverage by split and failure class, including single, fused, bridged, spiro, and mixed ring systems.
  • Quotient and invariance: prove that rates over canonical molecular successors are invariant to atom permutation, automorphisms, and alternative presentations of the same executable rewrite. On enumerable small states, verify that the factorized marked lift and exhaustive alias-aggregated chemical generator produce the same successor set and total successor rates.
  • Executor audit: report attempted, masked, rejected, and committed events by family; verify exact reversible round trips and teacher-successor support under the production inference fiber.
  • Target-free inference audit: log that the sampler receives no endpoint, program, oracle, or repair stage and uses the same source marginal and executor as training.

E1 · Unconditional baseline — prove the generator, don't win here

GuacaMol distribution learning, with MOSES or ZINC as a secondary transfer check. Developmental checkpoint decisions may use 2,000 samples; final FCD and coverage use at least 10,000 independent ancestral samples per seed, at least three seeds, a frozen ChemNet pipeline, and bootstrap confidence intervals. The gate for advancing to conditional is not best-in-class FCD—it is pathwise validity, diversity, faithful marginals, coverage, and absence of obvious topology pathology.

Locked GrIDDD QED reproduction

Use the same 800 starting molecules, 20 candidates per start, starting-QED band 0.7–0.8, and success definition: at least one candidate with QED in [0.9, 1.0] and fingerprint Tanimoto similarity ≥0.4 to its start. Match preprocessing, QED implementation, fingerprint type and parameters, proposal/oracle budget, selection rule, random seeds, and the all-attempt denominator. Report paired bootstrap confidence intervals and every invalid, duplicate, failed, or abstained attempt rather than silently removing it.

E4 · The valid-and-connected mechanism — test why it helps

Do not assert the causal result in advance. Measure reward improvement per oracle call, per proposed event, per committed molecule, and per wall-clock second under matched compute. Compare the hard legal fiber with a carefully matched proposal-plus-rejection variant, logging invalid, disconnected, duplicate, and abstained states. The mechanistic hypothesis is that valid-state guidance spends a larger fraction of its budget on meaningful oracle evaluations; the ablation determines whether that actually produces the sample-efficiency gain.

E5 · Transport and teacher-ambiguity analysis — validate the scoped prediction

Bin training states by remaining program length, effective legal branching, number of progress-equivalent successors, and teacher successor entropy. Measure teacher mass, learned successor rank, family accuracy, calibration, and downstream recovery success. Compare a single canonical program, randomized programs, and causal multi-successor supervision. Test where the 1/Meff approximation holds and report where it fails.

E6 · Ablations

Source/coupling: null or single atom vs independent carbon tree; primitive delete/regrow vs Graft; size-matched vs flexible-size source. Coordinated-event mechanism: match each Graft or whole-ring macro to a primitive micro decomposition under the same endpoint pairs and supervision budget; report certified program length, successor/teacher entropy, recovery accuracy, convergence, wall-clock and oracle cost, compute-normalized sampling quality, and optimization performance. This tests an inductive-bias hypothesis—not an incapability claim about per-coordinate models. Rings: scalar micro construction vs atomic whole-ring systems; empirical topology base measure vs uniform. Learning: single-program vs randomized/causal teachers; exact successor objective on small states vs factorized marked lift; with/without recovery curriculum. Control: filtering vs inference-time rate guidance vs reward fine-tuning across target rarity.

E7 · Editing capability — prove the promised transfer

Similarity-constrained property optimization, scaffold-preserving decoration, and a formula/size-changing edit task. Report success, similarity, property improvement, validity at every state, event counts, scaffold violations, and operator use. This block can be secondary in the main paper, but at least one convincing editing result is required before COMPOSE is described as a unified generator/editor.

Locked ICLR evidence contract

The paper must establish one model spanning credible unconditional de-novo generation, direct property targeting, similarity-constrained optimization, and editing. The distinctive empirical claim requires competitive targeting/optimization under matched budgets together with 100% committed-state validity/connectivity, higher usable-oracle efficiency, pathwise constraint adherence, and anytime valid candidates. Required mechanism evidence is matched Graft-versus-primitive, whole-ring-versus-primitive, hard-legal-support-versus-proposal/rejection, and empirical-base-rate-versus-flat-rate ablation. Required correctness evidence is exhaustive small-state equality between the factorized marked lift and the canonical alias-aggregated successor generator. Inference-time QED guidance is an engineering gate, not a substitute for the direct conditional and matched optimization tables.

06

Two-paper program — lipids are their own story

The same engine carries two papers at two venues. This one introduces and validates the structured molecular RGM construction; the companion applies it to a real translational problem with prospective in-vivo validation. Keeping them separate lets each make its strongest claim without diluting the other.

Paper 1 · this doc

COMPOSE — methods (ICLR)

Rewrite Generator Matching, the validity-closed molecular CTMC, transport-aware teachers, sufficient unconditional distribution learning, and headline conditional generation/optimization on public benchmarks. Establishes methodological credibility.

Paper 2 · companion

COMPOSE-Lipid — application (Nature Biotechnology)

A lipid-adapted rewrite generator with prospective synthesis and in-vivo validation for lung delivery, comparing frozen-oracle selection with validity-preserving guided generation. It is built independently from the COMPOSE RGM engine and an audited, reaction-diverse lipid corpus. Establishes translational impact.

Why the split is the right call

The methods paper owns the structured molecular stochastic-rewrite/Generator-Matching construction, its bridge and support guarantees, and public-benchmark evidence. The lipid paper owns lipid chemistry support, heterogeneous data, oracle validation, synthesis, formulation, in-vivo enrichment, and functional cargo. Lipids are not assumed easy: their size, repeated tails, ionization states, symmetry, and formulation confounding create a different set of problems. The two papers share a frozen core, not a duplicated claim set.

Evidence boundary

The sibling rank_d500k model's reported FCD 9.96 is not a COMPOSE/RGM result. The latest developmental flexible-size COMPOSE checkpoint produced 2,000/2,000 valid, unique, and novel samples under restricted neutral C/N/O/F chemistry with at most 40 heavy atoms. Against 5,000 held-out references, its full-support FCD was 16.08 (finite-sample bootstrap interval 15.61–16.54); on the matched neutral C/N/O/F≤40 support it was 11.29 (10.82–11.76). The gap is concentrated in covariance, aromatic/fused-ring coverage, and flexible-chain overrepresentation. That checkpoint remains a developmental rescue asset, not the frozen GuacaMol result: its presentation-level self-Grafts are handled by CTMC thinning rather than reported as chemical edits, and its residual size/ring calibration is still being measured. Every final claim is populated only from a frozen successor-semantics manifest selected by the prespecified gates; a fresh from-scratch run is required only if bounded calibration and conditional qualification fail.
