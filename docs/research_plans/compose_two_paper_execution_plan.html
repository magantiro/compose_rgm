The operating decision

Locked program

Paper 1 makes stochastic chemical rewriting plus Generator Matching the methodological contribution, uses unconditional generation as a strict sufficiency gate, and puts its empirical weight behind conditional generation and optimization. Paper 2 keeps the original six-figure translational story. It does not require Paper 1 publication, but our chosen execution policy starts lipid model training only after COMPOSE clears a credible unconditional-generator gate and one decisive conditional/optimization proof.

Do prove

A real generator

Target-free ancestral sampling, credible distribution learning, diversity, coverage, no topology pathology, and 100% committed-state validity/connectivity.

Do headline

Conditional design

Property targeting, valid-successor guidance, recovery-trained editing, and similarity-constrained optimization under matched oracle budgets.

Do not do

Die on FCD

Use FCD as one frozen diagnostic. Reopen the base for diagnosed coverage, covariance, size, ring, or collapse failures—not for endless incremental metric shaving.

Dependency versus chosen order

Scientifically, Paper 2 does not depend on Paper 1 submission or acceptance. Operationally, we choose a staged sequence: Paper 1 unconditional sufficiency → recovery/guidance machinery → QED optimization proof → lipid kernel and base training → LNPDB-lung oracle and Arms A/B → prospective lock. Lipid source acquisition, provenance, corpus auditing, and the LNPDB lung-endpoint schema may proceed now, but no lipid generator training or frozen oracle campaign is launched before the QED go-signal. This is risk control, not a shortcut: both papers retain their complete experiment programs.

04

Workstreams and activation order

A · Shared core

RGM semantics and infrastructure

Freeze executor, exact teacher replay, alias quotient, dense marked-rate model, source/coupling contract, ancestral sampler, operator/time diagnostics, throughput, and reproducibility.

B · Paper 1 base

Unconditional sufficiency

The fresh quotient formulations failed their bounded learning gates and are retired. Qualify the restored step-6,250 pancake checkpoint under lossless symmetry thinning, calibrate only the measured size/ring-rate failures, run a fixed 2,000-sample diagnostic, and retrain from scratch only if this bounded rescue fails. Full multi-seed public confirmation remains a publication task rather than a prerequisite for the first conditional smoke.

C · Paper 1 headline

Conditional and optimization

Implement valid recovery supervision, property conditioning, local valid-successor guidance, KL-anchored reward fine-tuning, and matched targeting/optimization protocols.

D · Paper 1 analysis

Formal contract and ablations

Prove closure, bounded-domain reachability, finite total hazard/non-explosion, quotient and presentation invariance, and bridge correctness; then measure effective program multiplicity and test source, matched macro-versus-micro, teacher, factorization, and hard-support mechanisms.

E · preparatory work active

Lipid chemistry and provenance

Now: acquire sources, define chemistry scope, audit licenses, canonicalize/deduplicate, create study/scaffold/linker splits, and separate molecule identity from formulation/cargo/route/dose/assay. Model training remains gated. Exact sources are inserted as they are verified.

F · schema now; training after P1-G7

LNPDB lung oracle and prospective design

Now: freeze the lung endpoint schema and audit eligible LNPDB rows. After authorization: train/calibrate the hard-split lung oracle, define AD/abstention, power the arms, confirm routes, and freeze formulation/QC, randomization, blinding, and endpoints. Non-lung rows do not supervise the oracle or reward.

Benchmark protocol registry

No benchmark by name only

Every result row receives a versioned protocol record before training. A published number is never copied as a comparator when the dataset, preprocessing, target grid, starting set, fingerprint, oracle, search budget, or denominator differs. When exact reproduction is impossible, the result is labeled contextual rather than head-to-head.
