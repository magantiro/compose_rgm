# Pendant-side decoration policy: scoped development contract

## Problem and distinct hypothesis

COMPOSE produces exact valid, fragment-preserving complete programs. The
seed-zero joint-completion pilot is still running and must remain unchanged.
Its six sealed decoration rows show 120/120 valid outputs, but only 29/120
pass the official joint QED/SA quality gate. Across those rows, selected
decoration endpoints average 32.0 heavy atoms and the pre-refinement plan
already averages 30.5. These observations are developmental, not a full
ten-prompt benchmark result. The same broad one-boundary catalog contains
both sides of a bond cut: 42,991 smaller-side and 41,503 larger-side training
occurrences; it contains no one-atom region. Therefore a legal one-boundary
region is not necessarily an appropriate *decoration*.

The new hypothesis is that scaffold decoration needs a one-sided pendant
content law and immediate stopping after the declared attachment interfaces
are satisfied. This is a distinct constraint-topology controller, not a
retuning of the frozen joint-completion law. Motif extension, superstructure,
linker/morphing, T4, PMO, and de novo are out of scope for this revision.

## Frozen data and proposal rule

Reuse exactly the 10,000 train-partition source molecules and row identities
of `diagnostics/fragment_training_region_catalog_v1`. Verify source and
catalog physical hashes before extracting anything. Do not use benchmark
drug structures, quality labels, QED, SA, or a held-out partition to select
content. For each non-ring single heavy-atom bond whose removal disconnects
a training molecule, retain only the smaller component (both orientations
when tied) as an observed rooted one-boundary region. This admits observed
one-atom terminals, acyclic branches, heterocycles, aromatic and aliphatic
rings without a motif table. Record excluded or unsupported chemistry rather
than silently changing the executor vocabulary.

Assign each training molecule unit total mass over its eligible cut-side
observations, so molecules with many cut bonds do not dominate. Group the
resulting content by the existing coarse boundary context and region size.
Within each declared decoration interface, sample a region from this
training-only law; condition all interface choices jointly on the 40-atom
capacity using exact dynamic programming, not rejection or a beam. Keep
positive support for every observed context-compatible region that fits.

Compile all selected regions into one complete COMPOSE program with the
existing shared `compile_region` and dependency/conflict-aware exact program
executor. Preserve the hard `RegionLock`, designated attachment conditions,
and final prompt check. The frozen (R_\theta) model scores the complete
legal candidates; use the current unit-temperature finite-panel softmax.
Stop when all requested attachments are complete. Do not append an unrelated
T4 refinement in this decoration-only arm, because the requested operation is
localized completion. Report that absence as an explicit policy difference,
not as use of the entire T4 proposal mixture. Eight candidate draws and the
same primitive/block/state budgets remain fixed.

## Falsifiers and execution boundary

Before quality evaluation, prove on all ten decoration prompts that the new
law can offer and execute legal programs, including one-atom and ring-bearing
content where context-compatible. Record each draw, refusal, primitive count,
source-row provenance, bound interface, final endpoint, model score, RNG state,
and exact graph/core/path checks. Require at least 90% attempt-level output,
100% connected chemical validity and exact prompt fidelity among outputs,
and no reduction in the declared 40-atom/32-primitive executor support.
If any of these fail, do not evaluate a headline metric or quietly add a
fallback. Preserve the negative gate.

If the zero-quality gate passes, run one 20-attempt-per-prompt development
pilot with the same official evaluator and frozen checkpoint, after the
current joint-completion pilot has sealed its ten-prompt result. The new law
must improve mean quality by at least ten points over that frozen decoration
arm while preserving uniqueness at least 90%, diversity at least 0.56,
attempt-level output at least 95%, and exact validity/fidelity at 100%.
These are development promotion gates, not a formal IVG/GenMol win. Evaluate
all ten prompts, keep null outcomes in denominators, and inspect representative
chemistry. Do not tune cut types, source balancing, selection temperature, or
size limits on the ten-prompt results. A failed gate is a reported negative.

No full official multi-seed benchmark, source checkpoint training, scored
oracle use, or change to any live pilot is authorized by this development
contract. Such a launch needs a separate decision after the development
evidence is sealed.
