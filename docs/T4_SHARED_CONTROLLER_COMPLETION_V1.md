## Scientific boundary

The proposed test asks whether one task-independent controller trained on all 77
locked T4 routes can generate useful complete molecules under the exact nine
source and fiber conditions above. Its primary output is a per-cell immutable
stream of generated molecules, evaluator receipts and terminal status. No result
or comparative claim exists in this preparation revision.

The future primary comparison is against the separately sealed frozen T4
comparators, loaded only by an offline reducer after this campaign is complete.
The already launched four-expert BRAF-v4 run has different policy support and is
operational history, not a duplicate replicate or interchangeable baseline. The
model support remains the locked COMPOSE representation, shared checkpoint,
three declared experts, exact sources and evaluator identities in the contract.

## Shared controller

Every cell uses the same three proposal experts and the same all-route checkpoint:

- shallow exploration, 480 draws and horizon 3;
- anchored replacement, 512 draws and horizon 3;
- scale-balanced complete-region proposals, pool 192, realization limit 96,
  beam and expansion width 48, at most four bindings and 4,000 expansions;
- batch 8, four parents, parent exploration 0.3, two exploration selections,
  two initial expert and route-scale floor rounds and value penalty 1.0;
- docking seed 20260919 and controller seed `2026091900 + source_global_index`.

The shared checkpoint is the task-independent 77-route, 147-region and
137-template artifact. Runtime cell payloads contain only their source adapter,
fiber, evaluator identity, shared settings, checkpoint identity, budget, receipt
policy and pending capsule requirement. They contain no benchmark registry,
reported comparator, teacher route, winner, previous score or legacy resume.

## Durability and accounting

The reusable cell runtime publishes immutable query locks and query reservations
before evaluation. Once a lock exists, missing or unresolved receipts wait until
the frozen deadline and then consume their locked budget exactly once as failures.
They are never resubmitted. Continuations use durable `reserved`, `running` and
`terminal` generations. One cell's state contains no campaign-global mutable
driver state.

Forty-nine is a hard ceiling, not a promise to replace candidate exhaustion. A
cell may terminate below 49 only by an explicitly reported exhaustion or failure.
No replacement or backfill is permitted.

## Remaining gates

Before a scored app can be added, all of the following require a later committed
revision:

1. Seal the concurrent actual-production zero-oracle support artifact for all nine
   exact cell/fiber pairs. Preserve any failed cell as a negative result.
2. Reconcile the already launched BRAF-v4 run through read-only durable receipts
   and establish that its calls are terminal. Do not resume it from this campaign.
3. Build a complete source capsule from one clean exact commit and pass the
   manifest verifier over every packaged file. Working-tree packaging is forbidden.
4. Seal a final self-hashed scored contract that binds the support artifact,
   source manifest and image identity.
5. Obtain a separate exact user authorization binding that final payload hash and
   the 441-call ceiling.

Comparator values may be loaded only by an offline reducer after current-run
candidate locks and scored receipts are durable.
