# De novo and fragment-constrained quality revision

Status: molecule-level diagnosis and one bounded zero-oracle development pilot, authorized by the user's 2026-09-23 request for direct generic repairs. No full official de novo or fragment benchmark result, PMO/T4 change, or superiority claim follows from this document alone.

## Identity and support

- Scientific problem: COMPOSE produces chemically valid complete molecules, but the post-ring-marginal de novo sample has low benchmark quality and fragment generation may bind valid edits to the wrong supplied interface.
- Primary outputs: (1) complete connected molecular graph endpoints from the broad-organic timed-CTMC de novo process; (2) complete exact-executed programs and endpoints conditioned on explicit retained-fragment interfaces.
- Central claim under test: generic, feasible placement and exact interface binding can improve quality and conditional success without relaxing committed-endpoint validity, graph preservation, uniqueness, or diversity.
- Setting: pinned de novo lineage-B checkpoint and GuacaMol training corpus for diagnosis; released ten-drug GenMol fragment prompts for a separate zero-oracle support/conditioning analysis. These are development analyses, not a held-out superiority claim.
- Baselines and causal comparisons: unchanged de novo arm A versus ring-marginal C1; C1 with versus without one prospective host-placement repair on matched trajectory seeds; unchanged fragment binder versus one prospective attachment-aware binder on identical prompts/seeds. Superstructure is a regression control. Linker tests first compare exact deterministic hand-constructed cases, not benchmark scores.
- Declared support: the pinned lineage-B de novo checkpoint uses the **C/N/O/F atom vocabulary**, not the newer broad-organic vocabulary. It samples exact persistent-slot graph states with up to 40 active atoms. The separate fragment proposal support is at most 32 primitives and eight blocks. Stereochemistry, S/P/Cl/Br atoms and arbitrary multi-neighbor birth are not implied for the pinned de novo checkpoint. The benchmark evaluator's accepted molecules and COMPOSE's executable support are distinct.

## Evidence and gates before intervention

The de novo artifact `diagnostics/denovo_ring_marginal_v1/arms_report_v1.json` at SHA-256 `b2dbd1ddcc3852372e0fb4f667f6bc7a7aaa6fcd94666657d0829f83b3cb234d` reports C1 on 150 attempted molecules: committed/parsed validity 150/150, uniqueness 150/150, quality 17/150, diversity 0.8896577814. It reports 319/393 planned ring systems realized (81.17%) and 74/393 refused for `no_host_support` (18.83%). C1 is incomplete against its 200-sample design target. The 81.2% number is realization, not refusal. These are computed historical results, not a new run.

The fragment protocol audit at SHA-256 `703a74ac4f4c7405de916bcb3327b39bab3d5b1731835a5a77cca0d4c5b0b465` defines 50 released prompts and separates generated-molecule validity from constraint validity. The zero-oracle one-proposal support panel at SHA-256 `d3258d8253a869db94f96a7033998896e246d83fecfd49d8f55d4a10ac678905` has 50/50 exact-valid constraint-satisfying deterministic proposals, but does not establish the 100-sample-per-prompt benchmark rate or quality. Any reported low fragment "validity" must be decomposed by denominator before a chemical-validity interpretation.

The historical scored fragment branch already tested attachment-aware binding. It increased motif and decoration task success but decreased diversity and some quality metrics on the same persisted committed-endpoint denominator. The current fragment track is therefore an isolated ablation of *post-coverage* interface restriction, not an attempt to rediscover attachment awareness. The historically scored linker sampler's direct core bond is a different path from the present deterministic support adapter; neither path's success can substitute for the other's benchmark result.

Saved C1 molecule inspection and exact size/CNOF-support-matched GuacaMol comparisons show a distributed quality defect after the ring-size repair: QED/SA failures have lower SA fragment scores, fewer aromatic rings, more nitrogen and rotatable bonds, and excess fluorine. In C1's QED-fail/SA-fail group, mean F atoms are 1.29 versus 0.57 in matched training molecules; 68.2% versus 25.9% contain F. The C1 quality-pass group does not have an F excess. The comparison is descriptive, not a causal fluorine effect. Four selected saved-seed replays must reproduce their exact original endpoints before any rule attribution is accepted; the initial replays implicate `atom_restate` in F births and `ring_system_grow` in partially unsaturated nonaromatic rings. These are selected traces, not population rates.

The C1 plan realizes 81.17% of requested systems. Its 18.83% host refusal is a separate bottleneck, but quality is not simply the complement of refusal: quality was 7/88 for fully realized plans versus 10/62 for incomplete plans in the saved sample. Both groups are observational and confounded; a host-placement fix must not be claimed as a quality fix without a matched trial.

Before changing a proposal law, inspect saved endpoint molecules and traces; classify quality failures and host refusals; pin official comparator/evaluator code and precise task semantics; locate and reproduce the alleged linker prompt defect in the *actual* scored harness. A separate support runner already constructs a nonzero linker path, so the defect must not be assumed to be in that runner without evidence.

Prospective falsifiers: a host repair must substantially reduce `no_host_support` among planned systems and improve final quality at matched seeds and heavy-atom strata without reducing 1.000 committed validity or materially losing diversity/uniqueness. An attachment repair must improve correct-site/full-task success on multiple prompts without lowering committed validity or superstructure performance. A linker repair must pass exact 2-, 3-, and 4-internal-atom path tests before any benchmark run. Failures remain reported, never silently filtered.

The narrow pilot in `configs/denovo_restate_prior_pilot_v1.json` is allowed after its implementation and inputs pass hash checks. It changes only the atom-restatement logit for F by a fixed -0.6, rounded from the support/size-matched corpus-to-C1 F-count ratio. It preserves all legal actions, the exact executor, the pinned checkpoint, the C1 ring plan, and every selected trajectory seed. It persists every attempted endpoint. The predeclared **signal**, not promotion gate, requires validity 1.000, uniqueness at least 0.95, diversity drop at most 0.03, at least 25% reduction in mean F atoms, and no more than 0.05 increase in strained-ring prevalence. Quality is reported but 24 pairs are underpowered for promotion; a fresh-seed confirmatory run is required if the pilot qualifies. A nonqualifying result is retained.

No target/drug-specific rules, target-SMILES guidance, beam search, post-hoc quality rejection, executor relaxation, live PMO or T4 modifications, scored benchmark launches, or Modal generation runs follow from this scope. Any further full scientific run needs a new exact self-hashed contract, clean committed source, and full molecule/provenance persistence.
