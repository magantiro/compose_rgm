## Identity and scientific question

COMPOSE supplies an executable marked molecular rewrite process with complete
supported molecular successors. The local/global region controller allocates
(parent, region, option) bundles; the option conditions primitive support and
the frozen committor guides valid execution. Ring and non-ring options remain
available, including generic. This is an augmented marked-law development
experiment, not a claim of exact full-scale molecular control.

Test whether the repaired production controller completes constructive programs
and offers useful molecules under the same T4 budget. Compare frozen committor
guidance with its option-conditioned reference and identical post-hoc selection.
PARP1 seed0, d=0.4 is inspected development data. Neither a better score nor a
successful ring proves held-out generalization or superiority over IVG.
