PROBLEM 1, panel (c): the arrow colours currently mean nothing
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
The legend says teal = "reference (frozen)" and orange = "control". But in the
panel each successor gets exactly ONE arrow, and the colours are mixed: y1 is
teal, y2 is orange, y3 is orange, y4 is teal, y5 is teal. As drawn this says
some successors are governed by the reference law and others by the control
law, which is wrong. Every successor is governed by both.

Fix it by drawing TWO arrows to each of the five successors instead of one:

  - a THIN TEAL arrow whose width is the reference probability, using the same
    five widths as the arrows in panel (b);
  - an ORANGE arrow drawn just beside it or slightly overlapping it, whose
    width is the controlled probability.

Make the orange widths clearly different from the teal ones, and specifically:
y3 must have a MUCH thicker orange arrow than its teal one, y5 must have a
much thinner orange arrow than its teal one, and y1 must be somewhat thinner
in orange than in teal. y2 and y4 stay roughly the same in both.

The reader should be able to see, successor by successor, that control keeps
the same set of successors but redistributes weight among them, promoting y3
and suppressing y5. Keep the legend exactly as it is.
