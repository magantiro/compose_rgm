# COMPOSE figure palette

Locked from the Figure 1 draft the author selected. Reuse verbatim in every
figure so the paper reads as one set.

## The organising idea

The palette is a WARM/COOL SPLIT, and the split carries meaning:

  COOL  (slate, teal, sage)   the process itself: what is possible, what is
                              plausible, the frozen reference law
  WARM  (sand, terracotta)    purpose: the goal, the budget, the controller,
                              anything the objective touches

That is why panel (c) is the only place terracotta appears in Figure 1, and
why it appears there heavily. Do not spend warm colour on anything that is
not goal-directed, or the distinction stops meaning anything.

## Rules

- Panel cards are NOT uniformly tinted. The panel about the pure reference
  process gets the cool card; goal-touching panels get the warm cream card.
- Bonds are one colour and one width, always.
- Arrow width carries magnitude; arrow colour carries which law is acting.
- Terracotta is scarce by design. One highlight per panel at most.
