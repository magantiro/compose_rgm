\section{Discussion and limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

We are explicit about scope.
Closure concerns connected, valence-admissible graphs under a declared vocabulary; it says nothing about synthetic accessibility, strain or oracle reliability, and an edit trajectory is not a synthesis route.
The vocabulary bounds the reachable chemistry --- charge is carried rather than designed, stereochemistry is not represented, and what no enabled family realizes is unreachable --- and exactness is claimed only where it holds: exact executor support, exact canonical normalization, exact dynamic programming on an enumerable reachable set, and the exact terminal reweighting of \cref{thm:doob} \emph{under an exact value}, while the deployed $h_\phi$ is amortized and approximate, never called exact.
The largest limitation is the shape of the evidence: one tier is measured, while the attribution ablation, the multi-objective head-to-head, retargeting, pathwise constraints and the Pareto fan are designed but unmeasured and appear as \XXX{}; the mechanism claim rests on an ablation not yet run, so the correct reading of \cref{sec:exp-competence} is that a future-aware controller works on this task, not that its forward-looking character is why.
What the construction does establish is that separating possible, plausible and purposeful lets purpose be replaced without retraining, and keeps control inside the chemistry the executor admits.
