# NoDistill repeated-topology macro gate, version 2

The route-free version-2 expert passed its frozen zero-oracle support gate and repaired the specific
PARP1-0/PARP1-1 gap found after attempt 1.

The expert generated 24 exact protected programs on each PARP source and JAK2-1. Every program used
12-23 primitives, added 8-19 heavy atoms, and added two or three cycles. All 96 generated endpoints
were unique and replayed exactly. The two BRAF sources correctly abstained because this additive
expert is a large-growth operation and those near-capacity sources cannot support its macro plans;
the existing prune/local experts remain responsible for that regime.

Before any teacher route was loaded, PARP1-0 contained three eligible large-growth multi-cycle
endpoints and PARP1-1 contained one. The four endpoints span 12-15 primitives, +10 to +13 heavy atoms,
and +2 to +3 cycles. This is the exact structural regime absent from attempt 1. The candidate locks
also demonstrate all six generic macro-plan compositions, including connected ring construction,
growth plus construction, replacement plus construction, open-and-reconstruct, and release-grow-
construct programs.

After all six locks were sealed, teacher descriptors were loaded for diagnosis. On PARP1-0 the v2
expert covered two exact, one coarse, and one semantic teacher descriptors versus zero for the
matched attempt-1 programs. On PARP1-1 it covered two exact, one coarse, and two semantic descriptors
versus zero. Across the entire matched panel it covered seven exact descriptors versus five for
attempt 1. Its lower aggregate coarse and semantic coverage reflects its deliberately narrow role as
an additive multi-cycle topology expert, not a replacement for the general small/medium/large
composer.

No exact teacher endpoint was recovered. No route template, fitted route weight, route identifier,
target/cell name, teacher endpoint, objective, or comparator score entered the proposal law. The
runtime function receives only a source graph, seed, and fixed work budgets.

All four eligible PARP1-0/PARP1-1 endpoints are sealed in
`scored_candidate_proposal.json`. That artifact is a proposal only and explicitly requires a separate
scored contract and authorization. No oracle, docking, Modal, or GPU call was made here.
