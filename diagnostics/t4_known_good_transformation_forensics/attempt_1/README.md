The distinction between structural region count and runtime support is material.
Every exact primitive trace can be segmented into repeated one- or two-event
local patches at the grammar level. However, the frozen hybrid controller invokes
the compositional generator only once at the source, then hands control to
Dynamic-v0. It does not repeatedly invoke the generator on descendants. Thus the
counterfactual repeated-patch result is not current runtime support and does not
establish proposal probability or Dynamic-v0 recovery.

Selector injection is intentionally absent. Injecting a 5–32-primitive teacher
route into a pool emitted by a one- or two-event generator would bypass the stage
whose support is under audit and would make a downstream rank look better defined
than it is.
