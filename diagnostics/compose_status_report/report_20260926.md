## 3. PMO secondary experiment (arm C) — appendix only

Arm C keeps the structured recipe intact (operation sequence, length, block boundaries, payloads and
every binding to an original source atom preserved byte-for-byte) and resamples only the operands
referring to atoms created by earlier operations, among the currently live created atoms that leave
the operation admissible.

AUC-Top10 paired against A over 17 completed cells: mean **-0.0201**, median -0.0119, A better on 11/17, t p=0.178, Wilcoxon p=0.145.

The intervention produced a smaller mean AUC reduction than the broad proposal replacement, with
substantial variation across objectives and runs. **This comparison does not resolve how much of the
full proposer's advantage is specifically attributable to these bindings**, and its effect must not be
treated as an additive fraction of the A/B effect.

## 4. Caveats that must travel with these numbers

1. **Nothing is a finished panel.** T4 replicates 2-3 and 2 arm-B campaigns are running; every
   unfinished value is best-so-far and can only improve.
2. **`gsk3b` is a reward-hackable ML-predictor oracle in every PMO arm.** Its leaders are off-manifold
   (QED 0.03-0.23, SA 6.2-6.6, bare phosphorus, stacked hydrazines). Not a chemistry result for any arm.
3. **The PMO effect is task-dependent**: clear on albuterol, gsk3b, ranolazine, celecoxib; neutral to
   slightly negative on isomers and scaffold_hop.
4. **B inherits arm A's synthesis-success filter, its realized edit length, and its arbitration random
   stream.** It replaces edit choices, construction priors and program reuse together; it does not
   isolate dependency scheduling.
5. **AUC-Top10 trapezoids from (0,0)**, so a 1,000-call AUC is structurally depressed ~5% and must
   never be compared to a published 10,000-call figure.
6. **T4 docking is not reproducible at the single-molecule level**: re-docking one molecule spans
   1.3 kcal/mol because `obabel --gen3D` is unseeded. Replicate s.d. on a cell mean is 0.46 (median),
   which is the right per-cell uncertainty; do not bold a single-run cell on a sub-1.3 margin.
7. **Two named phases sit inside the T4 COMPOSE columns**: the revival arm (cells re-run after the
   round-0 preemption window) and, in replicate 1, several support-expansion cells.
8. **T4 GraphGA/RetMol coverage is thin** (2-10 cells, older combined table); verify provenance or drop.
9. **No PMO arm's proposal-synthesis path is in the contract `implementation_sha256`.** Arm identity
   rests on the launch receipt `git_commit` plus the deployed image.
