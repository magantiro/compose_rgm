This is development evidence, not the final frozen full-benchmark result. Every included campaign used one docking seed. Older Full-146 winners are deliberately excluded from the current-controller score count.

The main near-term controller work is therefore narrow: repair sequential complete-region support and the 5HT1B protonation/candidate-exhaustion path, then complete BRAF and the missing delta 0.4 campaigns under the same shared controller. PMO begins only after the T4 objective is resolved, and no T4 ablation suite is planned.
