# NoDistill generic complete-program composer gate, attempt 1

This frozen zero-oracle gate passed its preregistered aggregate support criterion, but it did not
fully repair the motivating PARP semantic gap.

The route-free composer generated 216 unique programs, 36 on each of six sources, with exactly 72
programs in each primitive-scale band. All 216 replayed exactly. The programs used 14 generic
operator families in 22 compositions; 117 were multi-module, 25 carried a created-handle
dependency, 96 combined release with reconstruction, 86 combined ring and non-ring operators, and
111 modified multiple source regions. Heavy-atom changes ranged from -7 to +15 and cycle-rank
changes from -1 to +1. Thirty-five endpoints were eligible under their cell's endpoint fiber.

The retained-core-only baseline generated 112 candidates, all deletion-only, with no created-handle
dependencies, constructed attachments, release-and-reconstruct programs, or ring-plus-non-ring
compositions. It produced 22 eligible endpoints.

After all six autonomous locks were sealed, the diagnostic loaded teacher descriptors. The composer
covered 5 of 22 exact teacher descriptors, 5 of 11 coarse descriptors, and 6 of 11 semantic
descriptors, compared with 3, 3, and 0 for retained-core-only. It recovered zero exact teacher
endpoints. These are support diagnostics, not optimization or docking evidence.

The PARP result is mixed and decisive. PARP1-2 gained teacher-scale support, but PARP1-0 and PARP1-1
had zero exact, coarse, and semantic teacher-descriptor coverage. Their known programs require large
growth with gains of two or three cycles. Attempt 1's generic composition schedules realized at most
one added cycle. Thus the candidate locks show that generic multi-module construction is executable,
diverse, and broader than pruning, while also localizing the remaining PARP failure to repeated
ring-construction or annulation support. A versioned attempt 2 must test that operation class without
changing or overwriting these locks.

No oracle, docking, Modal, or GPU call was made.
