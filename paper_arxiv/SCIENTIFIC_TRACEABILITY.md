structure, but no filled endpoint-quality, likelihood, accuracy, benchmark,
Pareto, or calibration result. The completed 16,000-step run is reported only
as a measured mark-objective diagnostic with 661,105 admitted training traces,
32 snapshots, unresolved ring-opening and Graft capability, and no selected
checkpoint. The already inspected final test cannot select or repair a model.
Editing-V2 remains training-unauthorized, and no de-novo checkpoint exists.

selection and the relevant provenance-validated experiments pass.
