\section{Experimental design}
\label{sec:experiments}

The experiments separate the framework's claims rather than asking one external
method to match every component. E2--E4 test the frozen editing prior; E5 tests
the canonical quotient; E6 tests exact finite-horizon control on an enumerable
slice; E7 tests conditional and dynamic design with the editing prior frozen;
and E1 evaluates a separate timed de novo checkpoint. Task definitions,
normalization, budgets, seeds, and decision rules are frozen before final
outputs are inspected.

\subsection{Shared protocol and accounting}

\paragraph{One molecular evaluator.}
Every experiment obtains legal marks, executor successors, persistent-slot
semantics, capability flags, and canonical aggregation from the same production
successor evaluator. Experiment code may wrap its output with a new probability
law or controller, but cannot reconstruct the molecular kernel independently.
A slow dictionary implementation that enumerates, executes, canonicalizes, and
groups marks is used only as a verification oracle.

\paragraph{Comparison types.}
We distinguish three comparisons. A \emph{law-only} comparison holds legal
successor support fixed and changes only probabilities. A \emph{support
ablation} removes only preregistered operator families. An \emph{external
baseline} may use a different representation and support, so it is compared on
task endpoints and compute without an artificial support-equivalence claim.

\paragraph{Compute accounting.}
The primary cost axis for controlled design is the number of property-oracle
calls. We additionally record forward passes, successors scored, particles
propagated, accepted edits, wall-clock time, accelerator hours, and peak memory.
A method that reranks many successors or propagates many particles cannot count
only its committed molecular states.

\paragraph{Model selection.}
The editing checkpoint is selected on the validation partition by
production-weighted canonical-successor NLL, with balanced-family successor NLL
secondary and held-source, scaffold, topology, calibration, and productive
rollout safeguards. Hazard calibration is reported separately and cannot
select a fixed-budget editing checkpoint. Final test data are used once after
the checkpoint and all controller hyperparameters are frozen.

\subsection{E2: learned versus uniform molecular transport}

E2 asks whether Generator Matching learns useful chemistry beyond a hand-built
legal move graph. The learned arm samples from the frozen
$P_\theta^{\mathrm{edit}}$. The control arm is uniform over distinct canonical
molecular successors,
\[
  P_{\mathrm{unif}}(y\mid x)
  =
  \frac{1}{|\mathcal N(x)|},
\]
using the identical executor, source, support, and edit budget. Uniformity over
raw marks is not a valid control because aliases would give some molecules more
mass.

The primary endpoints are held-out canonical-successor NLL, analogue-target
recovery at matched budget, and path overhead relative to the registered
compiler or shortest verified path. Secondary endpoints include productive
displacement, immediate reversals, longer cycles, net versus gross edits,
endpoint fidelity, and oracle calls per recovered target.

\subsection{E3--E4: cardinality and topology adaptation}

\paragraph{Cardinality.}
E3 includes growth to a larger atom-count interval, shrinkage to a smaller
interval, a mid-trajectory switch from growth to shrinkage, held-out count
bins, and sources whose targets are unreachable under fixed cardinality.
Insertion-disabled, deletion-disabled, and fully fixed-cardinality support
ablations isolate which operators are load-bearing. We record every
intermediate atom count, overshoot, correction, edits to reach the interval,
source similarity, validity, and connectedness.

\paragraph{Topology.}
E4 includes cycle creation, cycle opening, ring-size change, topology
simplification, fused-system modification, and topology addition followed by
reversal after an objective switch. Spiro or bridged cases are included only
when the frozen production support and task builder certify reachability. The
main ablation disables primitive cycle operations while holding all other
support fields fixed. The obsolete whole-ring growth macro is not restored
merely to manufacture a baseline.

\subsection{E5--E6: quotient and exact-control verification}

\paragraph{Canonical quotient.}
E5 compares raw mark multiplicity and aggregate successor mass under
persistent-slot relabeling and deterministic within-fiber refinement. It checks
sampled successor frequencies, successor-controlled laws, and a fresh
dictionary oracle. A negative control applies mark-level power and top-$k$
rules, for which refinement invariance is expected to fail. The final result
artifact must be generated from the frozen production registry; the bounded
development fixture cannot fill the paper table.

\paragraph{Enumerable control slice.}
E6 uses the registered \texttt{carbon\_6\_slots} verification graph: 967
canonical states and 14,432 directed canonical edges under the production
executor semantics, with a distinguished null source. It contains birth,
death, topology change, multiple atom counts, multiple cycle ranks, and
extensive path multiplicity. It is deliberately dense carbon-only algebra, not
representative drug-like chemistry and not the production vocabulary.

For each remaining budget, we construct a row-stochastic base kernel, compute
the exact backward values, and apply \eqref{eq:finite-doob}. The primary metric
is total-variation residual against the analytic terminal tilt. Support
violations, backward residual, path enumeration on a smaller sub-slice, hard
unreachability, and exact dynamic continuation are audited separately.

\subsection{E7: conditional and dynamic molecular design}

All same-base controllers use one frozen editing prior, executor, source panel,
successor support, budgets, objectives, and oracle implementation. The
registered arms are unguided prior sampling, endpoint reranking, greedy
one-step reward, local Boltzmann reweighting, scalarized guidance,
MOG-DFM-style rank-directional guidance, SMC/Feynman--Kac control, and the
learned remaining-budget value. The comparison therefore isolates the control
law rather than conflating it with a different generator.

\paragraph{Pareto protocol.}
Two-objective tasks provide interpretable front plots; three-objective tasks
provide the stronger evaluation; a larger-objective task is a stress test.
Objective normalization, epsilon-dominance, similarity floors, and hypervolume
reference points are frozen before results. Primary metrics are normalized
hypervolume and hypervolume area under the oracle-call curve. IGD+, feasible
nondominated count, source similarity, diversity, and performance under an
independent held-out evaluator are secondary.

\paragraph{Dynamic protocols.}
Preference switching compares continuation from the current molecule, restart
from the original source, restart from the switch state with a separate
sampler, and a static compromise objective. The Pareto fan branches one valid
prefix toward multiple target regions and measures endpoint spread,
hypervolume, prefix reuse, and cost relative to independent restarts.
Pathwise-control tasks enforce scaffold, pharmacophore, atom-count, charge, and
structural-alert constraints at every committed state, with endpoint-only
filtering as the matched waste baseline.

\paragraph{External context.}
GraphGA and MARS are strong graph-search and valid-editor baselines
\citep{jensen2019graphga,xie2021mars}; RetMol and GenMol provide modern
retrieval and discrete-diffusion lead-optimization context
\citep{wang2023retmol,lee2025genmol}. An external row is included only when its
released artifact supports a compatible source set, objective, constraint,
budget, and standardization protocol. MOG-DFM is primarily ported as a
controller over the COMPOSE successor kernel rather than treated as an
architectural antagonist \citep{chen2025mogdfm}.

\subsection{E1: separate timed de novo generation}

E1 is intentionally last in the dependency graph because it requires a
separate broad-organic checkpoint trained with a nonzero de novo mixture and a
calibrated marked hazard. Sampling begins from the registered source
distribution, includes the reversible null source, and uses Gillespie timing.
The editing checkpoint is never substituted.

The primary distributional endpoints are Fr\'echet ChemNet Distance, held-out
chemical-space recall, and ring/topology distribution distance. All-step
validity, endpoint validity, connectedness, and uniqueness are hard gates.
Novelty, diversity, scaffold coverage, memorization, size and physicochemical
distributions, and throughput are reported secondarily.
