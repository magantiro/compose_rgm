\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

This draft fixes the result layout and interpretation rules before final
measurements exist. Red \textsf{XXX} tokens below are filled only from frozen,
provenance-validated artifacts; they are not estimates.

The completed 16,000-step editing run covered 661,105 admitted training traces
and produced 32 snapshots, but no snapshot is selected. Mark-level diagnostics
were substantially stronger for cycle closing than for cycle opening, which
remained poor, and the inherited bond-reroute diagnostic, historically called
Graft, collapsed between initialization and the final checkpoint.
The inspected final-test aggregate is barred from selection and repair, and the
run had zero de novo mixture weight. It therefore fills none of the learned
transport, adaptation, control, or unconditional-generation cells below.
